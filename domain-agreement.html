1. Overview

This Domain Registration Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between AXT TECHNOLOGYS PRIVATE LIMITED ("AXTCloud", "Registrar") and you ("Customer", "Registrant"). By registering, transferring, or renewing a domain name with us, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by all terms and conditions of this Agreement.

You also agree to be bound by the rules, regulations, and dispute resolution policies of the Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN) and the relevant registry for the top-level domain (TLD) you are registering.

2. Registration & Accuracy

2.1 Accuracy of Information

As a condition of registering a domain name, you are required to provide accurate, complete, and current information in the WHOIS directory. This includes your full name, postal address, email address, and telephone number.

2.2 Verification

We may be required to verify your contact information via email. Failure to respond to verification requests within the stipulated timeframe (usually 15 days) may result in the suspension or deletion of your domain name as per ICANN regulations.

3. Fees & Renewals

3.1 Payment

All fees for domain registration, transfer, and renewal are due in advance and are non-refundable. AXTCloud reserves the right to adjust pricing for domain services at any time without prior notice, although we generally attempt to notify customers of significant price changes.

3.2 Auto-Renewal

If you have enabled auto-renewal, we will attempt to charge your payment method on file prior to the expiration date. It is your sole responsibility to ensure your payment information is current and that your domain is renewed before it expires.

3.3 Expiration & Redemption

If a domain expires, it may enter a grace period followed by a redemption period. Renewing a domain during the redemption period often incurs significant additional fees imposed by the registry.

4. Domain Transfers

You may transfer your domain name to or from AXTCloud subject to ICANN's Transfer Policy. Transfers may be denied if:

  • The domain is within 60 days of initial registration or a previous transfer.
  • The domain is subject to a UDRP proceeding or court order.
  • There is a pending bankruptcy proceeding regarding the domain holder.
  • There is a dispute over the identity of the domain holder.

5. Dispute Resolution

You agree to be bound by the Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy (UDRP). If a third party challenges your right to use a domain name, you will be subject to the provisions specified in the UDRP.

AXTCloud will not act as an arbiter in disputes regarding domain ownership. We will implement decisions made by UDRP panels or competent courts.

6. Privacy & WHOIS Protection

We offer WHOIS Privacy Protection services for eligible TLDs. This service replaces your personal contact information in the public WHOIS database with proxy information. However, we reserve the right to reveal your identity to law enforcement, or to comply with a UDRP proceeding or court order.

7. Termination & Revocation

AXTCloud reserves the right to suspend, cancel, or transfer your domain name registration if:

  • You breach this Agreement or the Acceptable Use Policy.
  • We receive a court order or arbitration award requiring such action.
  • You use the domain for illegal activities, spam, or phishing.
