Cancellation & Refunds

Transparent policies regarding service cancellations and money-back guarantees.

Cancellation Policy

Customers have the right to cancel their services at any time. Cancellations must be submitted through the Client Area to ensure security and verification.

When cancelling, you may choose between two types of cancellation:

  • Immediate Cancellation: The service will be terminated immediately, and all data will be permanently deleted. This action is irreversible.
  • End of Billing Period: The service will remain active until the paid period expires, after which it will be automatically terminated.
Data Loss Warning: AXTCloud is not responsible for data loss once a cancellation request is processed. Please download all backups before cancelling.

Refund Eligibility

We offer a limited Money-Back Guarantee for new customers to test our services. Refund requests must be submitted via a support ticket within the specified timeframe.

Eligible Services (3-Day Guarantee):

The following services are eligible for a full refund within 3 days (72 hours) of the initial order:

  • Cloud VPS (Linux & Windows)
  • Minecraft Hosting
  • FiveM Hosting
  • Web Hosting

Non-Refundable Services:

Due to the nature of costs involved in provisioning, the following are strictly non-refundable:

  • Dedicated Servers: Due to physical hardware allocation and setup labor.
  • IP Addresses: Additional dedicated IPs.
  • Software Licenses: (e.g., cPanel, Windows Server, Plesk).
  • Domain Names: Registration fees are paid directly to the registry.
  • Setup Fees and Migration Fees.

Payment Methods

Refunds are processed to the original method of payment whenever possible.

  • Credit Cards / PayPal / UPI: Eligible for direct refunds to the source account.
  • Cryptocurrency: Due to exchange rate volatility and transaction fees, crypto payments are generally non-refundable. In exceptional cases approved by management, refunds may be issued as Account Credit only.

Abuse & ToS Violations

Our Money-Back Guarantee is void if a user violates our Terms of Service (ToS) or Acceptable Use Policy (AUP).

No refunds will be issued if:

  • The service was terminated due to illegal activity (Phishing, Fraud, Scanning).
  • The service was used for prohibited activities (Mining, DDoS attacks, Spam).
  • The IP address was blacklisted due to client actions.
