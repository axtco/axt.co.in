Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information regarding the delivery of digital services and activation timelines.

Nature of Goods

AXTCloud (AXT TECHNOLOGYS PVT LTD) provides exclusively digital products and services, including but not limited to Virtual Private Servers (VPS), Dedicated Servers, Game Hosting, and Web Hosting. We do not sell or ship physical products.

As such, there are no physical shipping charges, handling fees, or logistics involved. All services are delivered electronically via email and our secure client portal.

Method of Delivery

Upon successful payment and verification, delivery of your service is fulfilled through the following methods:

  • Email Confirmation: You will receive a "Welcome Email" containing your server IP, root/admin credentials, and initial setup instructions.
  • Client Portal: Your service will become "Active" in your AXTCloud dashboard, allowing you to manage, restart, and configure your server immediately.

Activation Timelines

We strive for instant activation, but timelines may vary based on the product type:

  • Cloud VPS / Virtual Servers: Instant Activation (Typically 1–15 minutes after payment).
  • Game Servers (Minecraft/FiveM): Instant Activation (Typically 1–10 minutes).
  • Dedicated Servers (Pre-configured): 1 to 24 Hours.
  • Dedicated Servers (Custom Config): 24 to 72 Hours (Subject to hardware availability and manual assembly).
Note: Activation times typically calculate from the moment payment is confirmed. Cryptocurrency payments may require blockchain confirmations before provisioning begins.

Verification Delays

To protect our customers and network infrastructure, all orders pass through an automated fraud detection system. In rare cases (approx. 1% of orders), an order may be flagged for manual review.

If your order is flagged, our billing team may request additional identity verification. Delivery of services will be paused until this verification is complete. If verification is successful, the service is provisioned immediately. If verification fails or is not provided within 48 hours, the order will be cancelled and refunded.

Non-Delivery

If you have not received your service credentials within the timeframes mentioned above, please check your email's Spam/Junk folder.

If the email is missing, please contact our support team immediately via:

  • Email: support@mail.axt.co.in
  • Ticket System: Available in the Client Area
